What we will not promotePhilippine Milk Code, EO 51

We do not promote

  • Anything that breaks the Philippine Milk Code (EO 51)
  • Products not registered with FDA Philippines
  • Claims that a product treats, prevents, or cures a disease
  • Doc A endorsing a product

Every paid post is marked as sponsored. Every product's FDA registration and claims are checked before posting, and we keep the right to say no.
